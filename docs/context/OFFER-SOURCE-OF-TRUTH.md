## Active Offer

### Local Call System: 90-Day Booking Foundation

**Founding price:** $499 per month
**Setup:** $0 for the first three accepted clients
**Billing:** Month-to-month
**Success horizon:** 90 days of implementation, stabilization, and evidence gathering without a long-term contract

### Positioning

Booked Out installs and manages the customer-facing conversion foundation that helps an established home-service company turn more of its existing demand into conversations, reviews, and booked-work readiness.

Do not lead with software, AI, generic SEO, or guaranteed lead generation. Lead with observable leaks between:

> Customer searches, trusts the company, makes contact, receives a timely response, books the next step, and receives a legitimate review request.

## Founding Scope

For one legal business/brand, one location, one domain, and one Google Business Profile:

- Managed mobile-first website and hosting, up to seven core pages
- Google Business Profile foundation audit and approved corrections
- Neutral review-request workflow by email, up to 100 eligible requests per month
- One primary lead form/inbox with routing and delivery tests
- Supported email acknowledgment and manual-first follow-up process, up to 50 inbound leads per month
- Two consolidated website revision rounds during implementation
- Monthly website maintenance within the approved scope
- One prioritized conversion improvement per month after launch
- Monthly evidence report and 30-minute Growth Desk review

## Explicit Exclusions

The founding scope does not include:

- Automated SMS, missed-call text-back, or cold SMS
- Paid advertising or media spend
- Guaranteed calls, leads, reviews, rankings, bookings, sales, or revenue
- Territory exclusivity
- 24/7 call answering or an AI receptionist
- Multiple brands, locations, domains, or Google Business Profiles
- Unlimited pages, revisions, content, design, or consulting
- Custom software, CRM replacement, or complex integrations
- Daily social-media management
- Full local SEO or service-area content programs

Additional scope requires a written change order or a separately approved service tier.

## 30-Day Foundation Promise

Once Booked Out has the required access, accurate business information, client assets, and timely approvals, the agreed core foundation will be completed within 30 calendar days.

If a Booked Out delay causes the agreed core foundation to miss that delivery commitment, the next recurring service month will not be charged until the missing agreed foundation is complete.

The promise does not apply to delays caused by missing client access or content, slow approvals, domain disputes, Google or other platform review, verification or enforcement, third-party outages, legal/compliance restrictions, or material scope changes.

This is a delivery promise, not an outcome guarantee.

## Qualification

Prioritize established, owner-operated, non-franchise HVAC businesses in one Austin-area metro cluster that have:

- A legitimate service history and customer base
- A verified or verifiable Google Business Profile
- Weak website conversion, inconsistent review velocity, or an unreliable follow-up process
- Enough completed work to support legitimate review requests
- Capacity to accept more work
- A responsive owner or operations manager
- Willingness to provide access and basic lead-status feedback

Disqualify businesses that need guaranteed immediate lead volume, cannot fulfill more work, routinely ignore inquiries, have unresolved licensing or service-quality issues, request fake reviews or prohibited tactics, or require SMS/advanced automation immediately.
