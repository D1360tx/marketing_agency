# Booked Out No-Brainer Offer Research

**Status:** Strategic recommendation, not yet the approved offer source of truth
**Prepared:** 2026-08-25 CDT

## Executive recommendation

Position Booked Out as a **managed revenue-capture and growth partner for established owner-operated home-service businesses**, beginning with HVAC companies in one Texas metro.

Do not lead with websites, SEO, software, AI, or generic marketing. Lead with the operating problem:

> Good local businesses lose work when customers cannot quickly find enough proof, understand why to choose them, reach them easily, or receive consistent follow-up. Booked Out installs and manages that customer-acquisition foundation with the owner.

The initial system should own controllable inputs and evidence, not promise revenue outcomes:

1. **Be found:** accurate Google Business Profile foundation and local business information.
2. **Be trusted:** current, authentic reviews and a credible conversion-focused website.
3. **Be easy to contact:** clear calls to action and functioning lead forms.
4. **Respond and follow up:** alerts, scripts, and managed follow-up workflows that are compliant with channel consent rules.
5. **Improve monthly:** evidence report, bottleneck review, and one prioritized growth action.

## What the research says

### SMB operating reality

BrightLocal's 2025 SMB marketing research reports that 54% of small-business owners manage marketing alone, only 35% report having a Google Business Profile, and only 40% have a dedicated website. The report identifies attracting new customers as the top priority and notes that profitability, cash flow, lack of time, and lack of expertise interfere with consistent marketing execution.

This supports a done-with-you / done-for-you partnership rather than another software login.

### Consumer decision behavior

BrightLocal's 2026 Local Consumer Review Survey reports:

- 97% of consumers read local-business reviews.
- 47% will not use a business with fewer than 20 reviews.
- 74% care primarily about reviews from the last three months.
- 31% will only use businesses rated 4.5 stars or higher.
- 85% are more likely to use a business after positive reviews.
- 54% visit the business website after reading positive reviews.
- 66% continue researching rather than booking immediately.
- 83% of consumers asked to leave a review went on to leave one in the survey year.

This validates the combined system: reviews alone are insufficient because consumers frequently move from reviews to the website for more proof.

### Price anchors

Current canonical vendor pages show:

| Provider | Public price | Important distinction |
|---|---:|---|
| NiceJob | Grow $75/month; Grow + Sites $174/month plus $199 setup | Reputation software and managed website, not full GBP/lead-response stewardship |
| Oak Harbor Web Designs | $175/month | Managed website only |
| GoSite | $299–$349/month | Productized website/local-presence tools; narrower human management |
| Podium | $399–$599/month | Lead-conversion and reputation software; HVAC pricing and usage fees can vary |
| HighLevel | $97/$297/$497 per month | Agency infrastructure that still requires configuration and fulfillment labor |
| Broadly | $799/month | Closest transparent done-for-you bundle: website, directory listings, reviews, social, and AI receptionist |
| Housecall Pro | $79/$189/$329 regular monthly plan anchors | Field-service software; websites and marketing products are separately quoted add-ons |
| Jobber | $49/$139/$199 regular one-user monthly anchors | Field-service software; several marketing functions are plan-dependent or add-ons |
| Scorpion, Hibu, Birdeye | Custom quote | Public sites do not provide a dependable current rate card |

The market separates into three broad bands:

1. **Software and productized tools:** approximately $75–$600/month, still requiring an operator.
2. **Managed website/local-presence products:** approximately $174–$349/month, with narrower scope.
3. **Integrated done-for-you service:** approximately $799/month and higher.

Booked Out at $499/month is therefore an intentionally discounted founding price, not a permanent full-service price. It is viable only with strict scope, manual-first delivery, direct founder learning, and a three-client limit. After measuring real implementation hours and support load, the evidence supports testing a mature managed price around **$899–$1,199 per location per month**, excluding advertising and usage overages.

## Recommended offer ladder

### 1. Free Revenue Leak Snapshot

A personalized, evidence-based audit used to open the conversation.

Include:

- Three observable leaks across Google presence, reviews, website conversion, or contact/follow-up.
- A simple mobile preview or annotated mockup only when it materially improves the prospecting conversation.
- One prioritized 30-day action plan.
- A 15-minute walkthrough call.

Do not deliver a full speculative strategy, fake performance estimate, or unsupported ROI projection.

### 2. Founding Partner: $499/month

**Limit:** first three accepted clients.
**Setup:** $0.
**Billing:** month-to-month.
**Success horizon:** a 90-day plan, without forcing a long-term contract.

Included:

- Managed mobile-first core website and hosting.
- Google Business Profile foundation audit and approved corrections.
- Authentic, neutral review-request workflow by email; consented SMS only after compliant infrastructure is active.
- Website lead capture, alerts, and owner/team response scripts.
- Basic follow-up process for inbound leads and open estimates where data and consent support it.
- Monthly edits, evidence report, and 30-minute Growth Desk review.
- One prioritized revenue-leak improvement per month, such as an estimate follow-up script, referral workflow, maintenance-plan positioning, service-page improvement, or intake correction.

Explicitly excluded:

- Ad spend and paid-media management.
- Guaranteed calls, leads, rankings, reviews, bookings, or revenue.
- 24/7 receptionist or call answering.
- Unlimited pages, design revisions, content, or consulting.
- Automated cold SMS or any SMS without affirmative consent and A2P readiness.
- Full CRM/field-service replacement.
- Daily social-media management.

### Risk reversal: 30-Day Foundation Promise

> Once Booked Out has the required access, accurate business information, and approvals, we will complete the agreed core foundation within 30 days. If we miss that controllable delivery commitment, the client is not charged for the next service month until the agreed foundation is complete.

Exclude delays caused by missing client access/content, third-party platform review, domain disputes, policy enforcement, or scope changes. This is a delivery guarantee, not an outcome guarantee.

### Founding scope boundaries

The $499 founding price must be limited to:

- One legal business/brand.
- One location, domain, and Google Business Profile.
- Up to seven core website pages.
- One primary lead form/inbox.
- Two consolidated website revision rounds.
- Email-only review requests and supported lead follow-up.
- Up to 100 eligible review-request emails per month.
- Up to 50 supported inbound leads per month.
- One prioritized conversion improvement per month after launch.
- Standard platform capabilities with no custom software development.

Additional locations, pages, rebrands, data cleanup, custom integrations, paid advertising, ongoing content programs, high-volume communication, photography/video, and major post-approval changes require a separate scope.

### 30/90-day delivery plan

**Days 1–5:** agreement/payment, access checklist, kickoff, baseline screenshots and tests, customer-path mapping, written implementation brief, and fit checkpoint.

**Week 2:** core website, service/service-area messaging, trust and CTA sections, acknowledgment/follow-up emails, and neutral review-request copy.

**Week 3:** consolidated revisions, form routing, GBP foundation changes, customer-list validation, and browser/mobile/form QA.

**Week 4:** deploy, test all conversion paths, launch eligible review requests, activate supported email follow-up, train the client's point person, and deliver the launch evidence report.

**Days 31–90:** observe real form/inbox behavior, correct routing and copy, continue approved review outreach, make one evidence-led conversion improvement monthly, repeat end-to-end tests, compare against the original baseline, and deliver a day-90 scorecard and next-quarter plan.

The scorecard must separate:

1. **Inputs completed:** deployed pages, corrected GBP fields, emails sent, follow-up steps, and tests.
2. **Leading indicators:** working submissions, response-time samples, delivery/response activity, legitimate reviews, and measurable CTA behavior.
3. **Business outcomes:** conversations, estimates, bookings, and known revenue, explicitly labeled as client-reported or attribution-supported.

### 3. Standard Managed System: test after the founding cohort

Do not lock this price until the first three clients establish actual implementation hours, revision load, support demands, and retention economics.

Recommended first post-cohort test:

- **$899/month plus $1,250 implementation.**
- Initial 90-day implementation/stabilization period, then month-to-month.
- Same core system with strict service limits, a defined monthly improvement cadence, and documented response/reporting standards.
- Keep advertising, telecom/AI overages, additional locations, and custom integrations separate.

The market supports a broader **$899–$1,199 per-location range**. Start at the bottom only if the founding cohort confirms efficient delivery. Booked Out must differentiate from Broadly's $799 AI-heavy bundle through hands-on local strategy, quality control, transparent evidence, client-controlled assets, and integration into the owner's real sales process.

### 4. Growth Partner: $1,499+ per month, defer

Do not actively sell until fulfillment capacity exists for expanded local SEO, citations, content, conversion testing, reactivation, quarterly planning, and faster support. Paid media must remain a separate scope and ad spend should never be included in the retainer.

## Unit economics framing

Do not claim that “one job always pays for it.” Use the prospect's own gross-contribution data.

Monthly revenue needed to recover the fee:

| Monthly fee | At 40% gross contribution | At 50% | At 60% |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| $499 | $1,247.50 | $998.00 | $831.67 |
| $899 | $2,247.50 | $1,798.00 | $1,498.33 |
| $1,199 | $2,997.50 | $2,398.00 | $1,998.33 |

Sales question:

> “After labor, materials, and direct job costs, how much contribution does an average booked repair, maintenance agreement, or installation create for you?”

Then calculate the break-even in their terms. Forbes' consumer cost guide lists average AC repair cost around $400 plus an average service-call fee around $150, with wide variation. This supports avoiding simplistic one-job claims, especially for low-ticket repairs.

## Qualification criteria

Prioritize businesses that are:

- Owner-operated and established, not franchise locations.
- HVAC first, then plumbing/electrical after the first vertical is proven.
- In one Texas metro cluster.
- Already completing enough jobs to request reviews consistently.
- Economically able to recover the founding $499 fee through approximately $832–$1,248 in additional monthly revenue at a 40%–60% gross contribution margin; mature pricing requires a correspondingly stronger operating base.
- Strong operators with weak or inconsistent website/GBP/review/follow-up execution.
- Willing to provide access, customer data lawfully, approvals, and a point person.

Avoid:

- Pre-revenue businesses.
- Businesses with severe service-quality/reputation problems.
- Prospects demanding guaranteed rankings/leads.
- Businesses unwilling to respond to leads or provide access.
- High-risk senders expecting bulk spam or non-consensual SMS.

## First outreach pilot

### Market

Start with one Austin-area HVAC cluster that can be served personally: Austin, Round Rock, Cedar Park, Leander, Georgetown, and Pflugerville. Do not mix verticals in the first campaign.

### List and cadence

1. Build a qualified list of 50 owner-operated HVAC businesses.
2. Score each on observable website, GBP, review-recency, trust, and contact-conversion gaps.
3. Prepare 15 high-quality Revenue Leak Snapshots; create previews only for the strongest prospects.
4. Send 10-15 manually approved emails per business day from the verified Booked Out domain.
5. Follow up on days 3, 7, and 14 with a new observation, not “just bumping this.”
6. Stop immediately on opt-out and suppress the address globally.
7. Track delivered, positive reply, audit accepted, meeting booked, proposal, close, and reason lost.

### Suggested positioning

Subject: `3 leaks I found on {{business_name}}'s customer path`

> Hi {{first_name}},
>
> I looked at how a homeowner finds and chooses {{business_name}} on mobile. I found three places where an otherwise good local company may be losing ready-to-book customers: {{specific_observation}}.
>
> I recorded a short Revenue Leak Snapshot showing what I found and what I would fix first. No generic SEO report and no obligation.
>
> Want me to send it over?
>
> Diego
> Booked Out

The first email should ask permission to send the audit rather than forcing a calendar link or pitching every deliverable.

### Compliance floor

Commercial email must use accurate sender/header information, a non-deceptive subject, a valid postal address, a clear opt-out, and prompt suppression. The FTC requires honoring opt-outs within 10 business days. Booked Out should continue using neutral review requests without gating, positive-sentiment requirements, or fake/incentivized-positive reviews.

## Current production blockers before outreach

Verified on 2026-08-25:

- `trybookedout.com` is live and the protected app redirects to login.
- Booking route returns 503 because `BOOKED_OUT_BOOKING_URL` is missing.
- Agreement route returns 503 because `BOOKED_OUT_AGREEMENT_URL` is missing.
- Payment route returns 503 because `BOOKED_OUT_LOCAL_CALL_PAYMENT_URL` is missing.
- Turnstile server secret exists but `NEXT_PUBLIC_TURNSTILE_SITE_KEY` is missing, blocking functional public CAPTCHA submissions.

Required before the first campaign:

1. Add matching Turnstile site key and redeploy.
2. Add and verify booking, agreement, and $499 recurring-payment destinations.
3. Run one controlled lead -> audit/preview -> email -> tracking -> booking -> agreement -> payment -> onboarding test using an owned address.
4. Confirm From/Reply-To inbox monitoring, postal footer, unsubscribe behavior, and suppression.
5. Keep SMS disabled until affirmative consent, A2P registration, STOP handling, and provider configuration are verified.
