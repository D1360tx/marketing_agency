# Booked Out Affiliate Program

## Status: Paused

Do not recruit affiliates or publish affiliate terms until Booked Out has at least three paying Local Call System clients and the direct-sales fulfillment dry run is complete.

## Proposed Economics, Subject to Approval

- Local Call System price: $499/month
- Proposed commission: $100/month for each active, paid referred client
- Commission begins after the referred client’s first cleared payment
- Commission stops when the client cancels, receives a refund, or becomes delinquent
- No self-referrals
- No paid advertising using the Booked Out brand without written approval

## Requirements Before Activation

- [ ] Affiliate terms reviewed
- [ ] Referral attribution implemented and tested
- [ ] Payout and tax-document process approved
- [ ] Refund, delinquency, and dispute handling defined
- [ ] Privacy disclosure updated
- [ ] Partner page updated
- [ ] Manual reconciliation tested for one full billing cycle

Until these gates pass, referrals may be handled only as individually documented introductions with no public recurring-commission promise.
